## Time series machine learning

I work on machine learning methods for ordered observations: sensor streams, human activity data, industrial measurements, biomedical signals, and other temporal data. My main focus is time series classification, with related interests in regression, clustering, segmentation, and anomaly detection.

## Collaboration interests

I am interested in collaborations involving temporal data, reproducible benchmarking, open-source machine learning, and applied sensor data problems. I am especially interested in projects where algorithmic development and domain evaluation can be tightly coupled.
